# Gentoo Dating Privacy Policy

*Draft for review. Placeholders are in [brackets].*

**Effective date:** [Launch date]

Gentoo Dating ("Gentoo", "we", "us") is a dating app run by For Happiness LLC,
a Georgia limited liability company. This policy explains what information we
collect, how we use and share it, and the choices and rights you have. It
applies to the Gentoo app and our website.

If you have questions, email us at **help@gentoodating.com** [placeholder].

## The short version

- We collect what you put in your profile, how you use Gentoo, and basic
  device information.
- Your profile is shown to other members. Your messages go only to the
  person you send them to.
- **We don't sell your personal information**, and we don't use your
  profile, your interests or who you're looking for to target ads.
- Ads in Gentoo are **non-personalized**: we don't send your profile details
  or your location to the ad network.
- You can see, correct, download and delete your information. Everyone gets
  these rights, wherever you live.

## 1. Information we collect

### Information you give us

| What | Examples |
|---|---|
| Account details | Email address and password |
| Profile | Name, birthday and age, gender, who you want to see, photos, bio, interests, prompt answers, job, home city, your match note |
| Preferences | Discover filters such as age range and maximum distance, the city you browse, notification and board settings |
| Activity with other members | Pebbles (likes) you give, the photo or answer each was for and any note you add; matches; messages, including voice messages; chat ratings |
| Community | Boards you create or join, posts and comments |
| Safety | Reports you make (and their reasons), blocks and unmatches |
| Support | Anything you send us when you contact us |

### Information we collect automatically

- **Location:** if you allow it, your phone's location, to show you people
  nearby and set your home city. Other members only see your city and an
  approximate distance, never your exact location.
- **Usage:** pebbles left, points, levels, challenge progress, ads watched
  for rewards, and when and how you use features.
- **Device and technical:** device type, operating system, app version, IP
  address, time zone (used to reset your daily pebbles at midnight) and
  crash information.

### Information from others

- **Other members**, for example when they report you or rate a chat.
- **Service providers** we use to sign you in and process payments (see
  section 4).

## 2. Sensitive information

Some of what you share is considered **sensitive personal information**
under some state laws, such as information that may reveal your sexual
orientation (your gender and who you want to see), your precise location
and your photos.

We use sensitive information **only to provide Gentoo to you**: to show you
to the right people, show you the right people, keep members safe and
prevent fraud. We do not use it to infer anything about you beyond that, and
we never use it for advertising. Because we use it only for these permitted
purposes, we don't offer a separate "limit the use of my sensitive
information" option.

## 3. How we use information

- **To run Gentoo:** create your account, show your profile, show you
  people in Discover, deliver pebbles and messages, and run matches, boards,
  challenges, levels and badges (such as Great Chat and Slow to reply).
- **To keep people safe:** review reports, block and remove people who
  break our Community Guidelines, prevent fraud, spam and ban evasion, and
  enforce our Terms.
- **For rewards and ads:** show ads and grant rewards for ads you choose to
  watch (such as extra pebbles), and enforce daily limits.
- **To communicate with you:** service messages, safety notices and, if you
  allow them, notifications.
- **To improve Gentoo:** understand how features are used and fix problems.
- **To meet legal obligations** and respond to lawful requests.

## 4. How we share information

- **With other members:** your profile (photos, name, age, bio, interests,
  prompts, job, home city and approximate distance) is visible to other
  members who can see you in Discover or in Pebbles. Your messages go to the
  person you send them to. Your board posts and comments are visible to
  members of that board. Your match note is shown only to people you match
  with.
- **With service providers** who help us run Gentoo and may use information
  only to do that work, including:
  - sign-in and account security (Clerk);
  - hosting, database and photo storage providers;
  - payments through Apple and Google (they process payments; we don't see
    your card details);
  - Google AdMob, to show ads (see section 5).
- **For safety and legal reasons:** to respond to valid legal requests,
  protect members or the public, investigate fraud, or enforce our Terms.
- **If our business changes hands,** for example in a merger or sale, your
  information may transfer to the new owner, who will be bound by this
  policy.

**We do not sell personal information**, and we do not share it for
cross-context behavioral advertising.

## 5. Ads

Gentoo shows ads through Google AdMob, including ads you choose to watch for
rewards and sponsored cards and posts. We request **non-personalized ads**
and do not send AdMob your profile details (such as gender, who you want to
see, age or interests) or your location. AdMob may still collect device
information such as your IP address and advertising identifier to show ads,
limit how often you see them and detect fraud. See
[Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and
[how Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
You can reset or limit your advertising identifier in your phone's settings.

## 6. Your choices and rights

Wherever you live, you can:

- **See and download** the information we have about you.
- **Correct** it (most of it directly in the app under Profile).
- **Delete your account** in the app (Profile > Pause or delete account),
  or by visiting gentoodating.com/delete-account. See section 7 for what
  happens next.
- **Turn off** location access and notifications in your phone's settings.
- **Not be discriminated against** for using any of these rights.

To make a request, email **help@gentoodating.com** [placeholder]. We may
need to verify your identity first, and we'll respond within 45 days. You
can use an authorized agent where the law allows. If we deny a request, you
can appeal by replying to our response.

## 7. How long we keep information

We keep your information while your account is open. When you delete your
account, we delete or anonymize your information within [30] days, except:

- information we must keep to comply with the law;
- information about reports and bans, kept to keep members safe and prevent
  people who were removed from rejoining, for as long as needed for that
  purpose;
- messages you sent remain visible to the people you sent them to until they
  delete their accounts, unless the conversation was ended by an unmatch,
  block or report.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information, such as
encryption in transit and access controls. No system is perfectly secure,
so please use a strong, unique password and tell us right away if you think
your account was compromised.

## 9. Adults only

Gentoo is only for people **18 and older**. We don't knowingly collect
information from anyone under 18. If we learn that someone under 18 has an
account, we'll delete it. If you believe a minor is using Gentoo, report
them in the app or email us.

## 10. Where we process information

Gentoo is run from the United States, and your information is stored and
processed in the United States.

## 11. Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we'll tell you in the app before they take
effect. The effective date at the top shows when this policy last changed.
