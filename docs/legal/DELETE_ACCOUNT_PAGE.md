## Delete your Gentoo account

This page is for **Gentoo** (Gentoo Dating), the dating app by **For
Happiness LLC**.

### The fastest way: in the app

1. Open Gentoo and tap **Profile** (bottom right).
2. Under **Account**, tap **Pause or delete account**.
3. Tap **Delete my account**, choose a reason if you like, and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away and you're signed out.

**Just need a break?** On the same screen you can **pause** your account
instead. New people won't see you, your matches and chats stay, and you can
unpause anytime.

### What we delete

- Your profile: name, age, birthday, gender, who you're looking for, bio,
  interests, job, prompt answers, match note and photos
- Your location and home city
- Your matches, chats and the pebbles you sent or received
- Your points, levels, badges, challenges and community board activity
- Your ad and reward history
- Your sign-in account

### What we keep, and for how long

- **Reports and bans:** reports you made, reports about you, and the record
  of a ban are kept as long as needed to keep members safe and stop
  removed people from rejoining.
- **Anything the law requires us to keep**, for as long as it requires.
- **Your reason for leaving**, if you chose one. It's saved without your
  name or any link to your account.
- **Messages you already sent** stay visible to the people you sent them to
  until they delete their own accounts, unless the chat was ended by an
  unmatch, block or report.

Everything else is deleted within **30 days** of your request.

### Subscriptions

Deleting your account **doesn't cancel a subscription**. If you have one,
cancel it in your phone's settings:

- **iPhone:** Settings > your name > Subscriptions, or
  [apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions](https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions)
- **Android:** Google Play > Profile > Payments & subscriptions, or
  [play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions)
