# Gentoo Community Guidelines

*Draft for review.*

Gentoo is for people who are serious about finding someone. These guidelines
explain what's welcome, what isn't, and what happens when someone breaks the
rules. They apply to everything on Gentoo: profiles, photos, prompts, pebble
notes, messages, voice messages, board posts and comments. They also apply
to how you treat people you met on Gentoo when you meet them in person.

## Be real

- **Be yourself.** Use your real first name, your real age and recent photos
  that clearly show you.
- **No impersonation.** Don't pretend to be someone else, a celebrity, a
  business or a made-up person.
- **No deceptive photos.** Don't use photos of someone else, or photos
  generated or heavily edited by AI to mislead people about who you are.
- **One account per person.** No fake, duplicate or shared accounts, and no
  bots or automation.

## Be respectful

- **No harassment or bullying.** That includes threats, intimidation,
  repeated unwanted contact, insults and unsolicited comments about
  someone's body.
- **No hate.** Don't attack or demean anyone for their race, ethnicity,
  national origin, religion, disability, gender, gender identity, sexual
  orientation or age.
- **Respect a no.** If someone doesn't reply, unmatches or blocks you, let
  it go. Don't try to contact them another way.
- **Respect privacy.** Don't share anyone's personal information, private
  photos or messages without their permission, and never post someone's
  address, workplace or contact details (doxing).

## Keep it appropriate

- **Adults only.** You must be 18 or older. We have zero tolerance for any
  content that sexualizes or endangers minors, and we report it to the
  authorities.
- **No nudity or sexual content** in profiles or board posts, and **no
  unsolicited sexual messages or images**.
- **No intimate images without consent.** Never share, or threaten to share,
  intimate images of someone without their consent, including fake images
  made with AI. This leads to an immediate ban.
- **No violence or self-harm content**, including graphic images and threats.

## No scams, sales or illegal activity

- **Never ask for money.** Don't ask other members for money, gifts,
  financial details or investments, whatever the reason.
- **No selling or promoting.** Gentoo isn't for advertising, recruiting,
  promoting other apps or social media for followers, or selling anything,
  including sexual services.
- **Nothing illegal.** No illegal drugs, weapons sales or other illegal
  activity.
- **No spam.** Don't send the same message to lots of people or post
  repeated links.

## Boards

- Stay kind and on topic in the boards you join.
- Report posts or boards that break these guidelines.

## Reporting and what happens next

- **Report anyone** who breaks these guidelines: from the menu (•••) in a
  chat, from their profile, or from a pebble note. Reporting someone also
  blocks them, so you won't see each other again.
- **We review reports** and may remove content, warn, suspend or permanently
  ban the account. Serious violations, such as threats, scams, intimate
  images without consent or anything involving minors, lead to an immediate
  ban.
- **We may act on behavior off Gentoo** too, if it involves people you met
  here.
- **Removed members may not come back** with a new account.
- **If you're in danger, call 911 first.**
