## App Store (Apple)

**Name** (30 characters max): Gentoo

**Subtitle** (30 max): Dating for real connection

**Promotional text** (170 max, can change anytime without a new build):
> Named after the gentoo penguin, which courts its partner with the perfect
> pebble. Five pebbles a day, so every like means something.

**Keywords** (100 max, comma-separated, no spaces needed):
> dating,date,relationship,singles,match,meet,serious,love,chat,partner

**Description:**

> Gentoo is named after the gentoo penguin, which courts its partner with
> the perfect pebble.
>
> **Why a penguin?**
> When a gentoo penguin finds the one, it searches the beach for the perfect
> pebble and offers it as a gift. If the pebble is accepted, the two build a
> nest together, and many pairs stay together season after season. On
> Gentoo, you give pebbles too, a few a day, to people you're truly
> interested in.
>
> **Make each pebble count**
> You get 5 pebbles a day. Give one on a photo or a prompt answer that
> caught your eye, and add a note if you like. Give a pebble back and it's a
> pair: now you can chat.
>
> **Conversations that go somewhere**
> People who reply and keep the conversation going earn the Great Chat
> badge. People who leave matches waiting get a Slow to reply badge, so you
> know what to expect.
>
> **Grow as you go**
> Daily and weekly challenges reward replying, real conversations and a
> complete profile. Level up to unlock free undo, an extra daily pebble and
> Priority.
>
> **Your safety comes first**
> Report or block anyone from any chat or profile, pause your account
> anytime, and read our dating safety tips in the app. Gentoo is for adults
> 18 and over.
>
> Gentoo is free, supported by ads.
>
> Terms of Service: gentoodating.com/terms
> Privacy Policy: gentoodating.com/privacy
