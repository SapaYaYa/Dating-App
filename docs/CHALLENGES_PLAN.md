# Challenges plan

Agreed November 2026. Replaces the placeholder challenges (the ones with a
"Mark Complete" button). Not built yet: most challenges can only be checked
once pebbles and chat are stored on the server (see the server work in
`OPEN_ITEMS.md`).

## Principles

- **Checked automatically.** No "Mark Complete" button and no honor system.
- **Reward good behavior:** replying, real conversations, thoughtful
  pebbles and a complete profile. Never the number of matches, and never
  just opening the app.
- **Priority takes about 3 months for an active user**, so the badge means
  something.
- Challenges that depend on something the person can't control only show
  when they apply (for example, "No One Waiting" only shows when someone is
  waiting on a reply).
- Points and pebbles from challenges have no cash value (Terms, section 7).

## Daily challenges

Reset at local midnight.

| Challenge | What counts | Points | Pebbles |
|---|---|---|---|
| Thoughtful Pebble | Send a pebble with a note of 20+ characters | 10 | +1 |
| No One Waiting | Reply to every match who's waiting on you. Only shows when someone is waiting; unmatching doesn't count as replying. | 15 | +1 |
| Say Hello | Send the first message to a new match. Only shows when there's an unopened match. | 10 | +1 |
| Join In | Comment on a board post. Only if community boards launch. | 5 | none |

## Weekly challenges

Reset Monday at local midnight (the placeholder screen says Wednesday; change
it).

| Challenge | What counts | Points | Pebbles |
|---|---|---|---|
| Real Conversation | A chat reaches 10 messages with each person sending at least 4 (the Great Chat rule) | 30 | +3 |
| Quick Replier | Reply within 24 hours to every match who messaged you this week (at least 3 people) | 25 | +2 |
| Steady Week | Send a pebble or a message on 5 of the 7 days | 20 | +2 |

## One-time milestones

| Challenge | What counts | Points | Pebbles |
|---|---|---|---|
| Picture Perfect | Have 5 or more photos | 25 | none |
| Tell Your Story | Write a bio and add 3 interests | 25 | none |
| Leave a Note | Set your match note | 10 | none |
| Stay Safe | Open the safety tips (Profile > Privacy & Safety) | 10 | none |
| First Pair | Your first match | 25 | none |
| Great Chat | Earn the Great Chat badge | 75 | +3 |

Milestones total 170 points (95 for the first five, which most people
finish in their first week or two, plus 75 for Great Chat).

## Levels

Early levels come quickly so new people feel progress; later ones take
longer. Replaces the flat 500 points per level. Level 5 (Legend) is the top;
points keep counting but there's no level 6.

| Level | Name | Total points | Unlocks |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pebble | 0 | none |
| 2 | Waddle | 250 | none |
| 3 | Huddle | 750 | none |
| 4 | Colony | 1,300 | free undo |
| 5 | Legend | 2,000 | 6th daily pebble and Priority |

## Pace (checked)

Assumptions:

- **Active user:** uses Gentoo 5 days a week. Sends a thoughtful pebble
  every one of those days; has someone waiting on a reply on half of them;
  has a new match to message on 30% of them. Finishes Steady Week every
  week, plus Real Conversation or Quick Replier (one of the two, on
  average).
  - Dailies: 5 × (10 + 0.5 × 15 + 0.3 × 10) = **102.5** a week
  - Weeklies: 20 + (30 + 25) ÷ 2 = **47.5** a week
  - Total: **150 points a week**
- **Casual user:** 2½ days a week. A thoughtful pebble each of those days
  and someone waiting on 30% of them; never Steady Week; Quick Replier in
  40% of weeks.
  - Dailies: 2.5 × (10 + 0.3 × 15) = **36.25**; weeklies: 0.4 × 25 = **10**
  - Total: about **46 points a week**
- **Does everything, every day:** 7 × (10 + 15 + 10) + (30 + 25 + 20) =
  **320 points a week** (355 with board comments).
- Everyone gets the first five milestones (95 points) early on.

Time to each level, (points needed − 95) ÷ points a week:

| Level | Active (150/wk) | Casual (~46/wk) | Everything (320/wk, with all 170 milestone points) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2 (250) | about 1 week | about 3½ weeks | under a week |
| 3 (750) | about 4½ weeks | about 3¼ months | about 2 weeks |
| 4 (1,300), free undo | about 8 weeks | about 6 months | about 3½ weeks |
| 5 (2,000), Priority | **about 12½–13 weeks** | about 9½ months | about 6 weeks |

Earning Great Chat (+75) takes about half a week off an active user's time
from then on (Priority in about 12 weeks instead of 12½–13).
